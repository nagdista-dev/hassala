## Full description

**Your words will not get lost. They get saved.**

**In one sentence:** Hassala is a free, open-source Chrome extension that
translates Arabic ↔ English and keeps every word in a private local word bank
with folders and export.

Hassala is not just another translator. It is your **personal word bank**: it
translates a word, saves it inside the extension, organises it into folders, and
lets you export it as Markdown, TXT, CSV, or JSON whenever you want.

### Why people choose Hassala

- **Translate without breaking flow:** a floating translator on any page, plus
  selected-text translation and interactive YouTube subtitles.
- **Keep what you look up:** one click saves the word and translation, and
  duplicate detection keeps the list clean.
- **Organise your words:** create folders such as English or Programming, choose
  where new words go, and move words between folders.
- **Export on your terms:** pick selected words, a folder, or everything; choose
  the format and filename; download. Nothing is written to your disk until you
  click export.
- **Own your data:** no account, no proprietary database, and no automatic file
  writes.

### What Hassala does

- **Instant Arabic ↔ English translation:** type a word or select text on any
  page and the translation appears automatically, with no button to press.
- **Save to your word bank:** one click stores the word and translation locally.
  Duplicates are skipped, so nothing gets repeated.
- **Folders:** create, rename, and delete folders. The built-in General folder
  is protected, and deleting another folder moves its words back to it.
- **Search and manage:** search words and translations, select individual words
  or a whole folder, move them, delete them, or clear the bank.
- **Export:** Markdown (`.md`), plain text (`.txt`), CSV (`.csv`), and JSON
  (`.json`), with a filename you choose.
- **Ready for the next word:** after a successful save the input clears
  automatically, focus returns to it, and a short confirmation appears.
- **Interactive YouTube subtitles:** hover a word to pause the video, click it
  to translate, or drag across a phrase to translate the whole phrase.
- **Floating button on every page:** translate while reading or studying
  without leaving the page.
- **Draggable panel:** move it anywhere on screen and pin it open when needed.
- **Site blocking:** disable Hassala on any site you choose.
- **Pronunciation and copy:** hear the word or translation, and copy either one.
- **Bilingual typography:** Baloo Bhaijaan 2 is bundled with the extension, so
  Arabic and English text look the same on every device.
- **Privacy first:** no accounts, no tracking, no analytics. Your words stay in
  local extension storage until you export or delete them.

### Getting started

1. Install the extension.
2. Click the Hassala icon or the floating button.
3. Type an Arabic or English word and press **Enter**.
4. Choose a folder, then click **Add to Hassala**. The input clears
   automatically so you can type the next word.
5. Open **Words** from Settings to organise, export, or delete.

### Your word bank

- Words live in `chrome.storage.local`, inside the extension.
- Nothing is written to your filesystem automatically. There is no native host
  and no Python requirement.
- The built-in General folder is always available and cannot be deleted.
- Limits: 10,000 words, 200 folders, 200 characters per word or translation.
- Export as Markdown to open in Obsidian, Logseq, Typora, VS Code, or any text
  editor. CSV is ready for Excel, and JSON is ready for backups or scripts.
- The export filename is sanitized, and the correct extension is added.

### Privacy

- Text you translate is sent to translation providers (Google and MyMemory) to
  return a translation.
- Saved words and folders stay in local extension storage.
- Exported files are downloaded only after you click export.
- No accounts, no tracking, no data selling.
- Full policy: https://gist.github.com/nagdista-dev/282cc2ce8fe35c5fb7199f7ffd2cd705

### Language support

- English-first interface with full Arabic and RTL support.
- Arabic ↔ English translation.
