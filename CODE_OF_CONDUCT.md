# Code of Conduct — Hassala

## Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in Hassala a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.
- Be kind to new contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Abusive, insulting, or threatening comments.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews, and any communication related to Hassala.

## Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying acceptable behavior and taking
appropriate, fair action in response to unacceptable behavior. They may remove,
edit, or reject comments and contributions that do not follow this code.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, use GitHub's **Report**
action or contact a maintainer privately on GitHub. All reports are handled
confidentially and with respect.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action appropriate to the situation, from a private
warning to a temporary or permanent ban from participating in the project.
